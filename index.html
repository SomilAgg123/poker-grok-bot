Tell Hold'em

How to play Tell Hold'em

Texas Hold'em, with one twist that changes everything: the Tell.

  1. You get three hole cards instead of two. Make your best 5-card hand from your 3 hole cards + 5 board cards.
  2. Betting is No-Limit: pre-flop, flop, turn, river, with blinds (they double every 8 hands).
  3. The Tell: after the flop betting, everyone still in must flip one hole card face up for the rest of the hand. You choose which. It still plays in your hand.
  4. Echo: if the card you flip matches the rank of a flop card, every other player still in pays you half a big blind. Showing strength gets paid — but it tells everyone you've got that pair.
  5. Bots read Tells: their equity math uses every revealed card. Hide your key card, show a decoy, or flaunt an ace to sell a bluff.
  6. Last player with chips wins. You're out when you bust.

Hand ranks (high→low): Straight Flush, Four of a Kind, Full House, Flush, Straight, Three of a Kind, Two Pair, Pair, High Card. A-2-3-4-5 is the lowest straight.
